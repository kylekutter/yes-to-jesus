About This Devotional

Where this content comes from, and how to go deeper.

You Said Yes was created to help people take their first steps after deciding to follow Jesus. Across 21 short readings, it explores foundational questions about God, the Bible, prayer, baptism, relationships, and living and loving like Jesus. You can move through it one day at a time, or at whatever pace helps you grow.

This website adapts that content into a simple, day-by-day reading experience for the web. Scripture references are powered by YouVersion, so you can read each passage in context and choose the Bible translation you prefer.

This is an independently maintained adaptation and is not an official YouVersion or Life.Church website. The original content is credited to Life.Church, with their permission to use and adapt it as a free resource.
