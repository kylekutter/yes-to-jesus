About You Said Yes

You Said Yes is a free resource from Life.Church, created to help you take your next step after saying yes to Jesus.

Why You Said Yes Exists

Saying yes to Jesus is the best decision you'll ever make—and it's the start of a lifelong journey, not a one-time moment.

Life.Church created You Said Yes to walk with you through that journey. Across 21 short readings, it explores what following Jesus actually looks like—how to talk with God, read the Bible, and grow closer to Him one day at a time.

Start where you are. Every day is simply another chance to take your next step closer to Jesus.

Three Ways to Experience You Said Yes

Read It on This Website

This website adapts You Said Yes into a simple, day-by-day reading experience for the web. Each day includes a short reading, Scripture, a prayer or reflection, and a practical next step.

Scripture references link to YouVersion so you can open each passage, read it in context, and choose the Bible translation you prefer.
