About This Devotional

Discover where You Said Yes came from—and choose the way you'd like to experience it.

Helping You Take Your Next Steps

You Said Yes was created to help people take their first steps after deciding to follow Jesus.

Across 21 short readings, it explores foundational questions about God, the Bible, prayer, baptism, relationships, and living and loving like Jesus. You can move through it one day at a time or at whatever pace helps you grow.

Following Jesus is a process of taking next steps—one day at a time.

Three Ways to Experience You Said Yes

Read It on This Website

This website adapts You Said Yes into a simple, day-by-day reading experience for the web. Each day includes a short reading, Scripture, a prayer or reflection, and a practical next step.

Scripture references link to YouVersion so you can open each passage, read it in context, and choose the Bible translation you prefer.
