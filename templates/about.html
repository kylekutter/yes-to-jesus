About “You Said Yes”

You Said Yes was created to help people take their first steps after deciding to follow Jesus. Across 21 short readings, it explores foundational questions about God, the Bible, prayer, baptism, relationships, and living and loving like Jesus. You can move through it one day at a time, or at whatever pace helps you grow.

About this website

This website adapts You Said Yes into a simple, day-by-day reading experience for the web. Scripture references link to YouVersion so you can open each passage, read it in context, and choose the Bible translation you prefer.

This is an independently maintained adaptation and is not an official YouVersion or Life.Church website. The original content is credited to Life.Church below, with their permission to use and adapt it as a free resource.
