About This Devotional

Where this content comes from, and how to go deeper.

This devotional's content is adapted from You Said Yes, a free resource from Life.Church's Open Network. Churches can download it here. You can also follow along day-by-day on the YouVersion Bible App.

This site (yestojesus.us) is an independent project built to make that same free content easy to read one day at a time, right in your browser — with scripture linked directly to YouVersion so you can read every passage in full context.

If you're a pastor, church staff member, or ministry leader and want the original, official, print-ready version of “You Said Yes,” visit Life.Church's Open Network at open.life.church — it's free for any church to use.

Why 21 days?

The devotional is organized into 21 days across three weeks — starting to follow Jesus, living like Jesus lived, and loving like Jesus loved — but you're welcome to go at whatever pace helps you grow. Finishing isn't the point. Finding direction is.

Scripture & YouVersion

Every scripture reference on this site links directly to the YouVersion Bible App / bible.com so you can read each passage in its full context, in whichever translation you prefer.
