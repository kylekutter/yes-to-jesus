Keep Growing

Saying yes to Jesus is the beginning of an incredible journey.

You're not alone on it. We've gathered some of our favorite resources from trusted ministries around the world to help you know Jesus, understand the Bible, build healthy spiritual habits, and keep growing closer to Him.

Start wherever you'd like. Every day is another opportunity to take your next step.

Keep Following Jesus

Remember, following Jesus isn't about having everything figured out.

It's about trusting Him one day at a time.

As you spend time in God's Word, talk with Him in prayer, connect with other believers, and put His teachings into practice, you'll discover that He is transforming your life from the inside out.

We're grateful to be part of your journey, and we're praying that God continues to lead you as you grow in your faith.
