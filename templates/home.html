You said yes to Jesus.
Now what?

A free, day-by-day devotional for new believers — real answers to the questions everyone asks after choosing to follow Jesus, with scripture from the YouVersion Bible App.

Start with Day 1 →

Inside, you'll discover more about what your decision to follow Jesus means. Each day includes a short reading from the Bible and something to try for yourself — answering questions like “what does this mean,” “what should I do,” and “what does this change?”

This is organized into 21 days, but there's no rush — go at whatever pace helps you grow. You might finish in a week, in 100 days, or keep coming back to it again and again. Finishing isn't the point. Finding direction, growing in confidence, and starting real conversations about what you're learning — that's what matters.

Read Along in the YouVersion Bible App

Every scripture on this site links straight to YouVersion, but the richest way to go through this devotional is inside the free Bible App itself — with your own highlights, notes, and daily reminders.
